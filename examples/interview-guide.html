How to use this

In each section, pick one or two questions or write your own, and ask them the same way in every interview. Rate the section right after the answer, using the anchor that fits best. Add notes, check the roll-up, then print or save as PDF. Nothing on this page is stored or sent.

Before you start

  • Ask every candidate the same core questions, so the answers can be compared fairly.
  • Keep every question tied to the job. If it would not help you judge how someone does this role, leave it out.
  • Never ask about age, family plans, religion, health, disability, national origin or any other protected trait. If a candidate raises one, steer back to the job and keep it out of your notes.
  • Take notes on the answers, what was said and done, and not on the person.
