Co-founder

Jenna Morrow

SHRM-CP. HR Generalist at The Lane Construction Corporation, and co-founder of TwoMorrows HR. Jenna's work is payroll precision, employee relations, and turning her own processes into the training guides whole teams run on.

Her background

Six years at Topgolf as Lead Administrative Assistant and Corporate Trainer. She owned the DSR tip allocation, checked the other administrators' work, and ran a parallel payroll reconciled with the office manager every cycle. At Adler Therapy Group, her written processes became the admin team's training bible. At Precision Measurements, she owned the payroll system conversion from Paycor to Paychex end to end and wrote the training guide her successor trained from.

Her systems

iCIMS, ADP, Workday, Phenom, Paychex, Deltek Vantagepoint, TheraOffice, and Litmos. DSR tip allocation, multi-state payroll, and process and procedure writing.

SAMPLE / MOCK DATA

Solution Command Center

What it replaced
Checking the ATS, the job board inbox, email and chat by hand every morning to build the day's call list.
How it works
One screen for a recruiter's whole book of business: who to contact today, every active candidate, every open req and a change log, refreshed daily. The sample below keeps the real structure and swaps in a pretend dragon daycare, so every name, number and pay rate is invented.
How it is fed
Fed by a sourcing tracker worksheet he built with live data connections to the team's recruiting systems, and by his own outreach templates for text, email, and messaging.
What he built
Not just the front end. The data plumbing is his too: the sourcing tracker worksheet, its live data connections to the team's recruiting systems (built on OData, an open data standard), and the outreach templates behind the Message builder.
The daily loop
Each workday starts from a fresh file. Work the queue top-down, mark what went out, and your own corrections ride over the next refresh.
